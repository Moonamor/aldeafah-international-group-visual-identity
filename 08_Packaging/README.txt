  AlDeafah_International_Group_Bag_M_320x400x120_CMYK+871foil.pdf   flat 900 × 515 mm   250 gsm art paper, matt lamination, black cotton rope

Sizes are width × height × depth (mm). Eyelets: matt metal, Ø 6 mm, 20 mm below the top fold.

File specification
  PDF/X-4:2010, output intent Coated FOGRA39, 3 mm bleed, trim marks, no fonts, no RGB objects.
  Plates:
    CMYK            Deafah Black ground 30/30/30/100 printed to the bleed (Brand Facts §Palette)
    PANTONE 871 C   = HOT-FOIL plate (gold): logo symbol (from the NEG-SOLID GOLD master, unchanged) + pattern strips.
                      Printer: use the foil closest to Pantone 871 C and send a proof before the run.
    Dieline         non-printing spot: solid = cut, dashed = fold. The dieline is GENERIC — replace it with the
                      factory's own dieline before production; the artwork positions are relative to the panels.
    Wordmark        the Arabic/English wordmark knocks out to paper white exactly as in the master (no white ink needed
                      on white art paper; if the factory prints on black stock, add an opaque white plate for it).

Artwork rules applied
  Front panel: logo NEG-SOLID GOLD, height = 30 % of the bag height, centred between the top fold and the strip.
  Front and back: pattern strip of whole 2 × 2 blocks at the master size (28.77 mm, master pitch), 25 mm above the
  bottom fold, ≥ 20 mm from the panel edges. Gussets, glue flap, top turn-over and bottom: black only.
  No text and no religious elements (the bag is discarded).

Generic dieline layout (left → right): glue flap 20 · front W · gusset D · back W · gusset D; top turn-over 40 mm;
bottom flap D/2 + 15 mm with diagonal gusset folds.
