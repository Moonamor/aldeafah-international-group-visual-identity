## Sound brief (for a composer — audio is produced separately)
- Length 4.0 s, starting at 0.5 s so it ends at 4.5 s, i.e. ≥ 0.3 s before the logo's hold begins to settle at 4.2 s is reached; nothing plays over the fade-out.
- One warm tone, not a melody: low strings (cello/viola register) swelling with the wipe (0.5–2.5 s), with a light metallic touch (a single soft bell or struck brass partial, no shimmer tails) landing when the Arabic name appears (2.5 s) to echo the gold.
- No rhythm, no percussion, no vocal, no religious sounds (no adhan, no recitation, no takbir), no stock "whoosh".
- Dynamics: −18 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; stereo, 48 kHz / 24-bit; deliver WAV + a −6 dB alternate for voice-over use.
- The film must still work in silence (most social feeds are muted): the audio is an addition, never a cue the picture depends on.
